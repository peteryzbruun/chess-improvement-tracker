# Fictional Weekly Review

> Fictional example. The player, games, and notes below are invented.

- Week: Example week
- Practice sessions: 3
- Games reviewed: 2
- Main focus: checking forcing replies before choosing a move

## Pattern noticed

In two reviewed positions, the example player chose a move before checking the opponent's checks and captures.

## What helped

Writing down candidate moves during one slow review made the missed reply easier to spot.

## Next week's experiment

Before committing to a move in one rapid game, pause and list the opponent's forcing replies.

## Reflection

The useful result this week was identifying a repeatable decision habit to practice. The example does not claim a rating increase.
